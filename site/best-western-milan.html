Where to stay: top Best Western hotels in Milan

Most Best Western Milan hotels sit north-east of the historic centre, within walking distance of the main railway station and the Corso Buenos Aires shopping street. That makes them practical for travellers arriving by train, including the Malpensa Express and Linate airport connections.

  • Best Western Hotel Madison: near Milano Centrale station, a short walk from the metro lines M2 and M3. A good fit for rail arrivals and early departures.
  • Best Western Hotel City (3-star): Corso Buenos Aires 42/5, about 1.6 miles from the city centre and 0.6 miles from Milano Centrale. Guest score 7.9/10 from 1,553 reviews; rates from €76 per night.

  • Best Western Plus Hotel Felice Casati (4-star): Via Felice Casati 18, near Corso Buenos Aires and Porta Venezia. Guest score 8.2/10 from 2,642 reviews; rates from €72 per night.
  • Best Western Plus Hotel Galles (4-star): Piazza Lima 2 on Corso Buenos Aires. Guest score 8.1/10 from 3,039 reviews; rates from €88 per night.
  • Best Western Hotel Astoria Milan (4-star): Viale Murillo 9, 0.9 miles from Fiera Milano City. Guest score 7.9/10 from 1,262 reviews; the lowest rate found, from €43 per night.
  • Best Western Hotel Mirage (4-star): Viale Certosa 104/106, north-west Milan. The highest guest score in this list, 8.8/10 from 1,910 reviews; rates from €54 per night.

How to find the best hotel deals in Milan

  1. Compare before you book. Rates for the same room can differ between booking sites, so check several at once.
  2. Avoid trade-fair weeks if you can. Prices rise sharply during large events at Fiera Milano, such as Milan Design Week in April and fashion weeks in February and September.
  3. Stay near Milano Centrale. Hotels around the station are often cheaper than the historic centre and are one or two metro stops from the Duomo.
  4. Check the cancellation policy. The lowest rate is sometimes non-refundable; a slightly higher flexible rate can be the better deal.
  5. Use Best Western Rewards. Members earn points on stays booked directly with Best Western, which can be worth comparing against the lowest third-party price.
