Kyoto hotels

By hotel class, 41 one-star hotels at JPY 10,517, 103 two-star hotels at JPY 17,930, 314 three-star hotels at JPY 15,618, 109 four-star hotels at JPY 24,019, and 26 five-star hotels at JPY 80,494 per night.
